A family story

Gardens built with care since 1996.

Founded by Paul Downing in Thames Ditton, Heritage Landscape Gardens brings decades of family know-how to landscape construction, soft landscaping and garden maintenance.

Paul's father first began the family business in 1961. Today Paul and his small team look after everything from everyday garden care to larger outdoor transformations.
