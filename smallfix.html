About Smallfix

Reliable practical help,
without the big-company fuss.

Smallfix is a London handyman service offering carpentry and joinery, general plumbing, electrical enquiries, decorating and garden maintenance. The existing site advertises free estimates and gives you Martin's number directly.

This independent concept keeps that straightforward approach and simply makes it easier for visitors to understand the services and describe the work they need.

A few helpful answers

Before you
get in touch.

Any job scope, price and scheduling are confirmed directly by Martin.

Can I ask about more than one job?

Yes. Select multiple categories in the job-list form and include the details in one email.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, the original Smallfix website advertises free estimates. Details and availability are confirmed directly.

Are electrical and plumbing jobs covered?

Smallfix lists these services. Suitability, competence, certification requirements and availability should be confirmed with Martin before work is agreed.

How does this website form work?

It opens your own email application with the selected details. This independent demo has no server-side submission system.
